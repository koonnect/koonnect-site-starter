A good starter does not try to solve every site on the internet.

It solves one class of sites very well, with a clear section catalog, structured content and enough flexibility for brand expression.

For Koonnect CMS, that means:

- **Astro** for the publishing layer
- **collections** as the editorial source of truth
- **stable targets** for the conversational layer

The result is a faster, cleaner and easier site to maintain.
